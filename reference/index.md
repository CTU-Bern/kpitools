- [`kpi_accumulate()`](kpi_accumulate.md) :

  Accumulate kpilists into KPIs per site lists The KPIs themselves are
  all well and good for e.g. a report where you walk through each
  individual KPI and present all of the info there, but they're not
  ideal if you want all of the KPIs for a given site or country or the
  overall study in a single table. `kpi_accumulate` does this conversion
